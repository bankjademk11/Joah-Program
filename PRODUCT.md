# Product

## Register
product

## Users
All employees across the organization (from store front-line staff to HQ executives and warehouse operators). Needs to be universally understandable regardless of technical skill level.

## Product Purpose
A centralized warehouse validation and store management system used for real-time inventory checking, data syncing between branches, and overall stock monitoring.

## Brand Personality
Professional, reliable, clean, easy on the eyes, and highly usable. Not overly flashy or distracting.

## Anti-references
- AI Slop: generic glowing borders, overused glassmorphism without purpose, excessive gradients, unrefined "default Tailwind" look.
- Unresponsive desktop-only layouts that break on mobile/tablets.
- Cluttered, overwhelming dashboards that cause cognitive overload.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Keep the interface clean and functional. Information architecture must be immediately obvious.
- **Universal responsiveness**: Must work seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
- **Professional restraint**: Use subtle shadows, purposeful white space, and a refined color palette rather than loud "AI" aesthetics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for readability, touch-friendly target sizes for mobile users, and a layout that naturally adapts without horizontal scrolling on small screens.
